### 1. For the **Port Operations User Profile** (`/dashboards/port-ops`)
The live maritime stream drives the **4-Stage Port Operational Manifest** in [`server/services/portOpsService.js`](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Downloads/Astra-main/Astra-main/SIH_2025---SIH26006-main/server/services/portOpsService.js):

1. **Incoming Vessels:** Real-time AIS pings for bulk carriers approaching the outer fairway. Displays live draft vs. harbor permissible draft.
2. **At Anchorage Queue:** Live count of vessels anchored at Paradip/Vizag roads with calculated average waiting hours.
3. **At Berth (Jetty Discharge):** Real-time monitoring of conveyor discharge rates ($\text{MT/hr}$) and estimated hours until berth clearance.
4. **Dynamic Operational Interventions:** If a vessel faces a swell delay or an anchorage queue exceeds 24 hours, the system recommends:
   - **Dynamic Feeder Swap:** Swapping a delayed Panamax with a standby coastal feeder to avoid idle crane penalties.
   - **Alternate Port Diversion:** Rerouting from Paradip to Dhamra or Krishnapatnam with instant road/rail fleet synchronization.

### 2. For the **Company User Profile** (`/dashboards/company`)
When an industrial buyer (e.g., Tata Steel or JSW) submits a **New Requirement** (`/new-requirement`):

1. **Live Dedicated Voyage Tracking:** Once **PLAN-01** is executed, the nominated ship (e.g., *MV Bengal Voyager*) appears on the interactive nautical map with real-time latitude/longitude, speed, and heading.
2. **Real-Time Demurrage Risk:** Compares the vessel's live ETA against the contractual laytime window. If sea weather causes a delay, it calculates the demurrage penalty ($/day).
3. **End-to-End Handoff:** The moment the ship berths at Paradip Port, ASTRA notifies the **TomTom Inland Fleet** to begin evacuation to the Angul plant.
